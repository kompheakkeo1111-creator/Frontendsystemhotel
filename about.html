Our Story

Welcome to Arizu Arimato

Arizu Arimato is a luxury boutique hotel located in the heart of Battambang, Cambodia's second-largest city. Housed in a beautifully restored colonial building, our hotel combines Khmer elegance with modern luxury.

Founded with a passion for hospitality, we offer personalized service that makes every guest feel at home. From our stunning swimming pool to our authentic restaurant, every detail is designed for your comfort.

Whether you're visiting Battambang for business or leisure, Arizu Arimato provides the perfect base to explore the city's rich culture, temples, and natural beauty.
